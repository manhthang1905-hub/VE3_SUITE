# SECTION 1: SUBJECT (S)

## 1.1 The 5-Layer Subject Formula

Build every subject description from inside out:

| Layer | Function | Family Drama Example |
|-------|----------|---------------------|
| **1. Identity** | Who — archetype, age, role | "A 31-year-old software architect with a quiet confidence" |
| **2. Physicality & Wardrobe** | Body + fabric + texture | "clean-shaven, wearing a navy crewneck sweater over collared shirt, silver wristwatch" |
| **3. Action & Interaction** | Dynamic verb + object contact | "gripping a phone to his ear, other hand pressing against the kitchen counter" |
| **4. Micro-Expression** | Eyes + facial muscles | "jaw tight with disbelief, eyes glistening with the first sting of betrayal" |
| **5. Hook Detail** | The one unforgettable element | "a perfectly set dining table for six visible behind him, untouched and candlelit" |

### Identity Keywords (Modern Family Archetypes)

| Category | Keywords |
|----------|----------|
| **Protagonists** | Tech professional, software engineer, startup founder, young architect, junior doctor |
| **Parents** | Controlling father, peacemaker mother, retired patriarch, widowed grandmother |
| **Siblings** | Golden child brother, favored sister, estranged twin, competitive older sibling |
| **Partners** | Supportive girlfriend, loyal husband, new fiancé, long-term partner |
| **Extended Family** | Wise grandfather, manipulative aunt, sympathetic cousin, disapproving in-law |
| **Supporting Cast** | Therapist, best friend, colleague, real estate lawyer, family attorney |

### Physicality & Wardrobe Keywords

| Element | Keywords |
|---------|----------|
| **Skin** | Natural skin texture, visible pores, slight stubble shadow, tired under-eyes, flushed cheeks |
| **Eyes** | Red-rimmed from crying, deep focus reflecting screen light, hollow exhaustion, sharp determination |
| **Hair** | Neatly combed professional cut, messy unwashed stress hair, grey-streaked at temples, loose ponytail |
| **Men's Clothing** | Navy crewneck sweater, fitted oxford shirt, charcoal wool coat, rolled-up sleeves, loosened tie |
| **Women's Clothing** | Cream cashmere cardigan, muted blouse, simple pendant necklace, oversized comfort sweater |
| **Casual/Home** | Faded college t-shirt, flannel pajama pants, bare feet on hardwood, bathrobe |
| **Accessories** | Silver wristwatch, reading glasses pushed up on forehead, phone in hand, coffee mug, car keys |

### Action Keywords (Physics-First — Natural Body Language)

| Action Type | Weak (Avoid) | Strong (Use) |
|-------------|-------------|--------------|
| **Phone Call** | "on the phone" | "gripping phone to ear with white knuckles, other hand bracing against counter edge" |
| **Crying** | "crying" | "collapsed forward on sofa, face buried in both hands, shoulders heaving silently" |
| **Arguing** | "arguing" | "leaning across the dinner table, index finger pointing, veins visible on neck" |
| **Waiting** | "sitting" | "perched on the edge of a kitchen chair, elbows on knees, staring at the floor" |
| **Walking Away** | "leaving" | "mid-stride toward the front door, hand already on the handle, not looking back" |
| **Reading** | "reading documents" | "hunched over legal papers at a desk lamp, pen frozen mid-note, mouth slightly open in shock" |

### Micro-Expression Keywords

| Emotion | Keywords |
|---------|----------|
| **Betrayal Shock** | Wide eyes, parted lips, frozen stillness, blood drained from face, thousand-yard stare |
| **Suppressed Anger** | Clenched jaw, flared nostrils, controlled breathing, white-knuckle grip, rigid posture |
| **Deep Sadness** | Downcast eyes, trembling lower lip, hollow cheeks, silent tears, exhausted surrender |
| **Inner Conflict** | Averted gaze, biting inner cheek, tension between brows, restless hands |

| **Determined Resolve** | Set jaw, steady forward gaze, squared shoulders, calm intensity in the eyes |

# SECTION 3: LIGHTING & CAMERA (N + C)

## 3.1 Domestic Interior Lighting Conditions

| Condition | Time/Source | Color Palette | Mood | Use For |
|-----------|------------|---------------|------|---------|
| **Morning Window** | Early morning | Soft white, pale gold, cool blue shadows | Fresh start, quiet hope | New day scenes, morning-after, waking up |
| **Golden Hour Interior** | Late afternoon | Rich amber, warm ivory, deep gold | Warmth, love, nostalgia | Family dinners, decorating, happy memories |
| **Overhead Harsh** | Midday or artificial | Flat white, strong shadows below eyes | Tension, interrogation, exposure | Arguments, confrontations, dinner disasters |
| **Single Lamp Night** | Evening, one source | Warm amber pool in darkness | Isolation, late-night crisis, rumination | Alone at desk, insomnia, reading documents |
| **Candlelight** | Indoor, candles only | Deep amber, orange flicker, soft shadow | Intimacy, expectation, vulnerability | Set dinner table, holiday prep, romantic |
| **Overcast Window** | Cloudy day | Flat cool light, desaturated, no shadow | Melancholy, emptiness, aftermath | Post-argument silence, mourning, contemplation |

### Lighting Direction for Emotional Mood

| Direction | Keywords | Family Drama Application |
|-----------|----------|--------------------------|
| **Window Side Light** | Soft directional light from window, gentle shadow on opposite face half | Intimate conversation, reflection, vulnerability |
| **Rembrandt Split** | Strong side light creating triangle of light under eye on shadow side | Inner conflict, moral weight, difficult decisions |
| **Overhead Flat** | Even overhead lighting, minimal shadows, slightly clinical | Arguments, tense dinners, uncomfortable truths |
| **Backlight / Rim** | Subject silhouetted, thin bright edge, background brighter than face | Walking away, isolation, looking out window |
| **Practical Lamp** | Warm pool of light from visible lamp in frame, darkness surrounding | Late-night alone, desk work, insomnia |
| **Soft Diffused** | Even, shadowless, overcast-quality light through sheer curtains | Healing moments, therapy sessions, calm conversations |

## 3.2 Camera Reference

### Shot Size

| Shot | Framing | Family Drama Use |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| **ECU (Extreme Close-Up)** | Eyes only, or hands on object | Tears forming, gripping phone, wedding ring, legal document text |
| **CU (Close-Up)** | Face, shoulders up | Emotional weight: crying, shock, realization, quiet anger |
| **MCU (Medium Close-Up)** | Chest up | Phone calls, one-on-one conversation, therapy session |
| **MS (Medium Shot)** | Waist up | Dinner table argument, kitchen confrontation, doorway standoff |
| **MWS (Medium Wide)** | Full body | Walking through house, sitting alone on sofa, pacing |
| **WS (Wide Shot)** | Full body + environment | Establishing the room, empty decorated space, approaching house |
| **EWS (Extreme Wide Shot)** | Subject small in environment | Walking alone in park, tiny figure in large empty house, city street |

### Camera Angle

| Angle | Keywords | Emotional Effect |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Eye Level** | Eye-level angle, neutral perspective | Empathy, witness POV, documentary intimacy |
| **Low Angle** | Low angle looking up, slightly heroic | Strength in defiance, standing ground, resolve |
| **High Angle** | High angle looking down, diminished subject | Vulnerability, defeat, overwhelmed, small in crisis |
| **Over-the-Shoulder** | OTS framing, seeing past one person to another | Confrontation, tense dialogue, power dynamics |
| **Worm's Eye** | Ground-level upward | Collapsed on floor, child's perspective, feeling crushed |
| **Dutch Angle** | Tilted frame, off-kilter | Shock moment, world turned upside down, disorientation |

### Lens & Focal Length

| Lens | Effect | Family Drama Use |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **35mm f/1.8** | Natural field of view, moderate background context | Wide establishing shots, rooms, exteriors, environment storytelling |
| **50mm f/1.4** | Human-eye perspective, flattering compression | Medium shots, dinner scenes, dialogue, two-person interaction |
| **85mm f/1.8** | Beautiful subject isolation, creamy bokeh | PRIMARY LENS — emotional close-ups, portraits, key dramatic moments |
| **24mm Wide** | Expanded space, slight distortion at edges | Large room establishing, crowd scenes, house exterior |
| **100mm Macro** | Extreme detail magnification | Ring on finger, tear drop, crumpled letter, key in lock |

### Depth of Field

| DOF | Keywords | Family Drama Use |
|-----|----------|-----------------|
| **Shallow (f/1.4-2.0)** | Shallow depth of field, dreamy bokeh, blurred background | Character isolation, emotional focus, intimate portraits |
| **Medium (f/2.8-4)** | Moderate depth, subject sharp with soft context | Balanced character + environment, dialogue scenes |
| **Deep (f/8-11)** | Deep focus, everything sharp | Establishing shots, empty room detail, architectural context |

### Camera + Lighting Mix Formula

```
[Shot Size] + [Angle] + [Lens] + [DOF] + [Lighting Condition] + [Light Direction]
```

# SECTION 5: SINGLE FRAME INTEGRITY

## Mandatory Rule

**Every image = ONE continuous frame = ONE moment = ONE location**

**Include in EVERY prompt:**

```
Single continuous photographic frame, one unified moment, no split screens, no collage, no text overlays, no graphic elements
```

## Subject Count Guidelines

| Subjects | AI Attention | Recommendation |
|----------|-------------|----------------|
| 1 | 100% — Maximum consistency | Best for emotional close-ups, solitary moments, phone calls |
| 2 | 50% each — Very good | Confrontation, couple walking, dinner conversation |
| 3-4 | 25-33% each — Good | Family dinner table, group argument, small gathering |
| 5+ | <20% each — Use background blur | Wide establishing shots, party scenes, crowd in café |

# SECTION 6: REFERENCE-ONLY RULE

## When Using Asset References in Prompts

| Asset Type | DO NOT Describe (Fixed) | DO Describe (Variable) |
|------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| **Characters** | Face shape, age, ethnicity, body type, hair color | Expression, clothing variant, pose, action, emotional state, grooming |
| **Interior Spaces** | Room layout, wall color, furniture placement | Lighting condition, time of day, atmospheric detail, emotional context |
| **Objects** | Object design, brand, model | Lighting on object, positioning in frame, wear/use evidence |
| **Recurring Settings** | Architecture style, floor plan | Weather through windows, season, mess level, decoration state |

**WHY (Reference-Only Rule above):** Prevents conflict between text description and reference image. AI generator follows the reference for fixed attributes and the text for variable attributes.

## No Proper Names Rule (Image Prompts)

**CRITICAL: Never use proper names in image prompts.** AI image generators (Google Nano Banana Pro, Gemini Image) prioritize metadata knowledge over reference assets when proper names are detected — overriding your visual references with the model's internal bias.

**Use visual descriptors + (ref ASSET_ID) instead:**

| Do NOT Use | Use Instead |
|-----------|-------------|
| "Ethan" | "The young man in navy sweater at the dining table (ref CHAR_001)" |
| "Tyler" | "The tech entrepreneur in charcoal coat (ref CHAR_002)" |
| "Jason" | "The software engineer at his desk (ref CHAR_003)" |
| "Rachel" | "The woman with dark hair in cream cardigan (ref CHAR_004)" |
| "Marcus" | "The older brother in the expensive suit (ref CHAR_005)" |
| "Richard" / "The father" | "The grey-haired father in polo shirt at the head of the table (ref CHAR_006)" |
| "Grandpa Walter" | "The elderly grandfather in the diner booth (ref CHAR_007)" |
| "Ethan's kitchen" | "The modern open-plan kitchen with pendant lights (ref ENV_001)" |
| "Tyler's Tahoe cabin" | "The three-bedroom lakeside cabin with wooden deck (ref ENV_002)" |
| "Jason's apartment" | "The modern condo unit with large windows and hardwood floors (ref ENV_003)" |
| "The legal documents" | "The property transfer notification on official letterhead (ref PROP_001)" |

**WHY:** Proper names trigger the AI's internal visual bias (stock photo associations, celebrity look-alikes, inconsistent character generation), which conflicts with your reference assets and the Contemporary Emotional Realism visual system.

# SECTION 8: CONTEMPORARY AUTHENTICITY CHECKLIST

Before finalizing any prompt, verify:

- [ ] Setting is modern and domestic (no historical, biblical, or fantasy elements)
- [ ] All characters wear contemporary clothing appropriate to their social context
- [ ] Materials and furniture are specifically named (not generic)
- [ ] Clothing matches the character's age, profession, and scene context
- [ ] Lighting is physically plausible for the indoor/outdoor setting
- [ ] Color palette follows the muted Portra 400 aesthetic (never oversaturated)
- [ ] Skin tones are natural and unaffected by overall color grading
- [ ] Emotional expression is specific and matches the narrative moment
- [ ] Single Frame Integrity maintained (one moment, one location, no composites)
- [ ] No AI-typical artifacts specified in negative prompt (extra fingers, text, plastic skin)
